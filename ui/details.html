How the final model was chosen

Recorded results from artifacts/ (model_comparison.json, selection.json, bayesian_trials.csv, tuning.json). Static: nothing here is computed live. Each step feeds the next.

1

Cross-validation of all 5 models

Same 13 features, same labels, same 3 trip-grouped folds of the 80 training trips. Gives each model a fair score so they can be compared.

Candidate macro F1. Big dot = mean of the 3 folds, small circles = the individual folds.

2

Choose the best

Rule: highest mean grouped-CV candidate macro F1. Student-test trips are not used to choose (student_test_used_in_selection: false).

Hist gradient boosting

Mean CV macro F1 0.9278 (± 0.0104) against 0.9260 for random forest, the runner-up. It was ahead on 2 of 3 folds. The margin is small (0.0018), so the top three are close.

3

Bayesian tuning of the winner only

12 Optuna TPE trials (4 random starts, then 8 guided), each scored by the same grouped cross-validation. 70 s in total.

Beforelearning_rate 0.1 · max_leaf_nodes 15 · l2_regularization 1.0 · min_samples_leaf 30CV macro F1 0.9278
After (best trial)learning_rate 0.0413 · max_leaf_nodes 18 · l2_regularization 0.343 · min_samples_leaf 60CV macro F1 0.9290 (± 0.0092), a gain of +0.0012
